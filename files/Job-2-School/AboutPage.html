Our Genesis

Our Three-Decade Journey

Founded in 1995 by an esteemed body of philanthropists and educationists, Ideal Model High School & College was envisioned as a beacon of modern enlightenment. Commencing with modest bamboo infrastructure, today the institution sprawls across a 5-acre scenic, eco-friendly campus featuring modern science, ICT, and robotics laboratories.

Over 15,000 alumni have graduated from this institute, moving on to prestigious engineering, medical, and public universities worldwide.

Prof. Mohammad Shahidul Alam
Principal & Headmaster (M.Sc, M.Ed)

Message From The Principal

Our Mission

To cultivate an inclusive, technology-integrated, and values-centered academic environment that inspires students to achieve cognitive excellence, creative problem-solving capabilities, and empathetic citizenship.

  • Foster STEM and critical inquiry
  • Preserve moral discipline and integrity
  • Ensure equal opportunity for all learners

Our Vision

To become a premier national landmark in secondary and higher secondary education, producing globally capable leaders, researchers, and innovators who contribute proactively to human civilization.

  • Smart digital campus infrastructure by 2030
  • Holistic character and leadership building
  • National athletic & robotics championships
