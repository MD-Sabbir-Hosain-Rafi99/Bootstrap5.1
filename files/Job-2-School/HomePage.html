Glorious History of Ideal Model High School

Established in 1995, Ideal Model High School & College commenced its journey with merely 85 students and 7 dedicated instructors. Over three decades of persistence, institutional integrity, and modern pedagocical standards, it has blossomed into one of the country's most renowned educational institutions.

Today, our campus houses over 3,200 eager learners across Primary, Secondary, and Higher Secondary divisions with 100% board examination success rates and top-tier national rankings.
